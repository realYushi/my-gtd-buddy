# GTD

A personal Getting Things Done workflow for capturing reminders, choosing next actions, and reviewing commitments.

## Language

**Reminder decision**:
A choice that changes a reminder's place or readiness in the GTD workflow, such as moving it, processing it into Next Actions, delegating it, or completing it.

**Disposition**:
The decision that moves an Inbox item out of Inbox: now, later, delegate, someday, or delete.

**Undo**:
Reversal of a reminder decision, restoring the values that decision changed to their previous values while preserving unrelated edits. A later edit to a value being restored creates an undo conflict; deleting a reminder is permanent and cannot be undone.

**Undo conflict**:
A reminder decision cannot be reversed because a value it changed has since been edited again. The reminder remains unchanged by the attempted undo.

**Available time**:
Time remaining today within the 9 AM–6 PM workday that is not occupied by timed calendar events; all-day events do not occupy available time. Overlapping events occupy their shared time only once, including events already underway.
_Avoid_: Free time today (when referring to the entire workday)

**Session outcome**:
The facts recorded about work performed during a GTD session, including processed items, deferred items, and context usage where known. A decision reversed before the session closes does not contribute to that session's totals.

**Processed item**:
An Inbox item given a disposition during a session, including moving it to Next Actions, Waiting For, or Someday, or deleting it. Skipped items and new captures are not processed items.

**Deferred item**:
An item moved to Someday. Deferring an Inbox item contributes to both the processed and deferred counts.

**Context usage**:
The number of reminders assigned a context, such as home, office, errands, or calls.

**Processing velocity**:
The average number of Inbox items processed per processing session. Coach activity does not contribute to processing velocity.

**Tracking week**:
A Monday–Sunday calendar week in the Mac's local timezone. Weekly history covers the current tracking week and three previous tracking weeks; missing observations are unknown.

**Completion snapshot**:
A count of reminders completed during the seven days preceding a review. Snapshots taken at different reviews may overlap and do not represent additive completion totals.
